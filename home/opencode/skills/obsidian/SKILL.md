# Skill: obsidian

## What I do

- Help you work with Obsidian notes and vaults
- Provide commands and workflows for managing your notes
- Assist with Obsidian shell commands and integrations

## When to use me

When you're working with Obsidian notes, vaults, or related tools.

## Key commands

- List vaults: `obsidian --list`
- Open vault: `obsidian --open-vault <vault-name>`
- Create note: `obsidian --create-note <vault> <note-name>`
- Search notes: `obsidian --search <vault> <query>`

## Notes storage

Obsidian vaults are typically stored in:
- macOS: `~/Documents/Obsidian/`
- Linux: `~/obsidian/`
- Custom locations configured in Obsidian settings

## Best practices

- Use consistent naming conventions for notes
- Leverage tags for organization
- Use linking between notes for knowledge management
- Regular vault backups are recommended
